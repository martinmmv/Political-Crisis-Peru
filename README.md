# Presidential Instability in Peru (1980–2026)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes patterns of presidential stability and turnover in Peru since the return to democracy in 1980. By examining how presidents enter and leave office, the project explores the institutional dynamics behind Peru’s contemporary political crisis.

Since 2018, Peru has experienced an unprecedented level of presidential turnover, with multiple presidents removed, forced to resign, or replaced through constitutional succession. This project uses data visualization to investigate how these patterns compare to earlier decades of the democratic period.

The analysis focuses on three key institutional dimensions:

- **Mode of Entry into Office** – how presidents came to power (election, constitutional succession, etc.)
- **Mode of Exit from Office** – how presidential terms ended (completion of term, resignation, congressional removal)
- **Term Duration** – how long each president remained in power

Together, these indicators help illustrate the changing dynamics of executive power in Peru.

---

## Data

The dataset includes presidential terms in Peru beginning in **1980**, when democratic rule was restored after military government.

Each row represents a **specific presidential term** and includes information such as:

- President
- Term start and end dates
- Historical period
- Mode of entry into office
- Mode of exit from office


---

## Methods

The analysis is conducted in **Python** using:

- **Pandas** for data cleaning and feature engineering
- **Matplotlib** for visualization

Two types of visualizations are used:

### Presidential Timelines

These charts display the chronological sequence of presidents and highlight periods of stability and rapid turnover.

### Horizontal Duration Charts

These charts compare the length of presidential terms, allowing quick identification of unusually short or long administrations.

Visualizations are generated by grouping presidencies according to:

- historical period
- mode of entry into office
- mode of exit from office

---

## Research Questions

This project explores several questions:

- Has presidential stability changed since the return to democracy?
- How common are non-electoral pathways to the presidency?
- How frequently do presidents leave office before completing their terms?
- What distinguishes the recent political crisis from earlier democratic periods?

---

## Key Insight

The visualizations reveal a clear structural shift after **2016**, with a dramatic increase in presidential turnover. While earlier decades were dominated by elected presidents completing their terms, the recent period shows repeated removals, resignations, and constitutional successions.

This pattern suggests a cycle of **presidential instability driven by executive–legislative conflict and weak party institutions**.
